# data/

Holds `MANIFEST.yaml` and nothing else that is tracked.

Corpus content is not checked in. Redistributable corpora are fetched by a
download script recorded in the manifest; non-redistributable corpora are
referenced only. The manifest is the record of what exists, where it came from,
what may be done with it, and what may not.

See `context/DATA.md` for the sourcing rules and `context/DECISIONS.md` D010
for the one corpus that may never be used for training.
